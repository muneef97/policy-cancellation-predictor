Question 3

How far ahead can we flag someone, and how often do we check?

The earlier we flag a policy, the more time the team has to act on it - but the signal gets a little less certain the further out we look. Score out of 100, where 50 is an even chance.

Monthly re-scoring keeps our 3-month window moving forward automatically.

Not a demographic artifact. WA churns lower in 6 of 7 acquisition channels (Broker is the exception, but WA's Broker sample is only 203 policies - too small to read into).
